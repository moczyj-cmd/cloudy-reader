▸ ABOUT CLOUDY READER
The Cloudy Architecture

Core Philosophy: Absolute Digital Ergonomics

Built to eliminate optical crowding, harsh screen glare, and reading fatigue. Cloudy Reader is a universal desktop environment engineered to make long-form reading, threat analysis, and study sessions completely frictionless.

100% Air-Gapped & Sovereign

Zero telemetry. Zero external CDNs. Zero tracking scripts. Every typeface, focus tone, and display adjustment executes strictly on your local machine—pure utility, absolute privacy, and zero network footprint.

Cloudy Reader is a personal client-side display and audio preference utility, not a medical device or a certified ADA / WCAG / Section 508 compliance overlay. All controls adjust mechanical display and acoustic parameters only and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any clinical condition. Use is entirely at your own risk; by using this extension, you agree to these terms.
