1. Zero-Telemetry Privacy Policy

Zero Data Collection Guarantee: Cloudy Reader does not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, IP addresses, or usage analytics.

100% Local Storage & Execution: All user preferences, custom typography presets, and scratchpad notes are stored strictly on your local storage hardware via chrome.storage.local. All bundled open-source fonts and speech synthesis routines execute locally on your device without pinging external font CDNs or remote telemetry servers.
