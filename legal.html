1. Zero-Telemetry Privacy & Permissions Policy

Zero Data Collection Guarantee: Cloudy Reader does not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, IP addresses, or usage analytics. Extension pages enforce a strict connect-src 'none' Content Security Policy that blocks external network transmissions.

100% Local Storage & Execution: All user preferences, custom typography presets, and Agenda Notebook entries are stored strictly on your local hardware via chrome.storage.local. Users are responsible for exporting important notes to Markdown (.md), as clearing browser data or uninstalling the extension will erase local storage. All bundled open-source fonts and Web Audio synthesizer routines execute locally on your device, and Text-to-Speech utilizes your browser's native Web Speech API (strictly prioritizing offline localService OS voices).

Browser Permissions Disclosure: Permissions declared in manifest.json (storage, activeTab, tabs, contextMenus, and host access <all_urls>) are utilized exclusively on-device to apply user-selected CSS/DOM styles, synchronize theme states across open tabs, and trigger local right-click Text-to-Speech.
